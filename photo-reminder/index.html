How reliable is this?

This is a web app, which keeps it easy to try on any phone, but browsers limit what an app can do once you leave it.

  • Timers: fire while the app is open or in a recent tab. If your phone fully suspends the tab, the alert appears the moment you come back.
  • Leaving a place: location is only tracked while the app is open on screen. Browsers don't allow background geofencing.
  • Notifications: need permission. On iPhone the app must be added to the Home Screen first.
  • Privacy: photos and locations never leave your device. Everything is stored in the browser.

For true background alarms and geofences, the next step is a native iOS/Android app or a push-notification server.
